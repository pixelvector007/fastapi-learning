Features

Everything you need to manage patients

Simple, validated and fast.

Full CRUD

Create, view, edit and delete patient records from the dashboard.

Automatic BMI

BMI and an Underweight / Normal / Overweight / Obese verdict are computed from height and weight.

Search & sort

Find patients by name, city or ID, filter by verdict and sort by age, height, weight or BMI.

Validated data

Pydantic models enforce limits on names, ages, heights and weights before anything is saved.

At-a-glance stats

Totals, average BMI and a verdict distribution bar update instantly as records change.

Light & dark

A responsive interface that works on phones and desktops, in your preferred theme.
